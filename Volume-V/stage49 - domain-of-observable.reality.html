🔶 2. MODULE-WISE MATHEMATICAL CORE

🔹 Module 1 — Measurement (Selection)

\langle O \rangle = \langle \Psi | \hat{O} | \Psi \rangle

\rho \rightarrow \rho_{\text{diag}}

👉 Selection = projection to definite outcomes

🔹 Module 2 — Decoherence

\rho_{ij}(t) = c_i c_j^* e^{-\Gamma_{ij} t}

\rho_{ij} \rightarrow 0 \quad (i \neq j)

👉 Loss of coherence = suppression of interference

🔹 Module 3 — Stability of Matter

\nabla V(x) = 0, \quad \delta^2 V(x) > 0

E_{\text{bound}} < E_{\text{free}}

👉 Stability = local energy minimum

🔹 Module 4 — Macroscopic Emergence

\langle A \rangle = \int A(\Gamma)\, \rho(\Gamma)\, d\Gamma

\frac{\Delta A}{\langle A \rangle} \sim \frac{1}{\sqrt{N}} \rightarrow 0

👉 Reality = statistical averaging

\mathcal{R} = \{ \langle \Psi | \hat{O}_i | \Psi \rangle \}

\mathcal{R} = \mathcal{F}(G, H, \Psi)

👉 Reality = structured measurable set

\boxed{ |\Psi\rangle \;\rightarrow\; \rho \;\rightarrow\; \rho_{\text{diag}} \;\rightarrow\; V_{\text{eff}}(x) \;\rightarrow\; \langle A \rangle \;\rightarrow\; \mathcal{R} }
