Prediction 1: Substrate-Induced Corrections to Gauge Coupling Running

  • Classification: Genuinely Novel Quantitative Substrate Prediction
  • Underlying Parameters: Vacuum Stiffness \(K=11.350\), Synthesis Pressure \(P=0.6900\), structural ratio \(\eta_{KP}=K/P=16.4493\), substrate scale \(\Lambda_{\mathrm{sub}}=109.72\,\mathrm{GeV}\), gauge-response coefficient \(\mathcal C_g(3\,\mathrm{TeV})=1.420\), and electroweak mixing angle \(\theta_W\).
  • Core Mechanism: The normalized unified substrate generator is \[ \widehat{\mathcal G} = K\widehat{\mathcal R} + P\widehat{\mathcal E}, \] with \[ K=11.350, \qquad P=0.6900, \] and structural ratio \[ \eta_{KP} = \frac{K}{P} = 16.4493. \] The characteristic substrate scale is \[ \Lambda_{\mathrm{sub}} = \frac{M_Z}{\sqrt{P}} = 109.72\ {\rm GeV}. \] The substrate contribution to the inverse gauge coupling is \[ \mathcal R_g(Q) = \frac{1}{16\pi^2} \frac{K}{P} \left( \frac{Q}{\Lambda_{\mathrm{sub}}} \right)^2 \ln\left( \frac{Q^2}{M_Z^2} \right) \mathcal N_g(Q), \] and the effective inverse gauge coupling is \[ \alpha_i^{-1}(Q) = \alpha_i^{-1}(M_Z) - \frac{b_i}{2\pi} \ln\left( \frac{Q}{M_Z} \right) - \mathcal R_g(Q). \] The corresponding electroweak mixing-angle displacement is \[ \Delta\sin^2\theta_W(Q) = -\mathcal R_g(Q). \] At \[ Q=3\ {\rm TeV}, \] the substrate contribution is \[ \Delta\sin^2\theta_W(3\ {\rm TeV}) = -(1.42\pm0.15)\times10^{-4}. \] Thus the substrate predicts a definite negative displacement of the high-energy electroweak mixing parameter relative to the Standard Model prediction.
  • Observable / Test: Precision measurements of high-energy neutral-current processes and electroweak asymmetries at momentum transfers near \(Q=3\,\mathrm{TeV}\), including forward-backward asymmetries, angular distributions, and effective determinations of the weak mixing angle.
  • Localized Falsification: Measurements at \(Q=3\,\mathrm{TeV}\) that determine the substrate-induced displacement with sufficient precision to exclude \[ \Delta\sin^2\theta_W = -(1.42\pm0.15)\times10^{-4} \] falsify this specific substrate-induced gauge-coupling-running prediction only. It does not, by itself, falsify Predictions 2 or 3.

Prediction 2: Substrate-Modified Bhabha Scattering

  • Classification: Genuinely Novel Quantitative Scattering Prediction
  • Underlying Parameters: Vacuum Stiffness \(K=11.350\), Synthesis Pressure \(P=0.6900\), structural ratio \(\eta_{KP}=K/P=16.4493\), \(Z\)-boson mass \(M_Z=91.1876\,\mathrm{GeV}\), equilibrium background interaction density \(\rho_b=PM_Z^2\), and center-of-mass energy \(\sqrt{s}\).
  • Core Mechanism: The substrate correction to the Bhabha-scattering cross-section is \[ \delta_{\mathrm{sub}}(s) = \frac{K}{P} \frac{\rho_b}{s}. \] Since \[ \rho_b=PM_Z^2, \] this becomes \[ \delta_{\mathrm{sub}}(s) = \frac{KM_Z^2}{s}. \] Consequently, \[ \frac{d\sigma_{\mathrm{Synthesis}}} {d\Omega} = \frac{d\sigma_{\mathrm{SM}}} {d\Omega} \left[ 1+ \frac{KM_Z^2}{s} \right]. \] At \[ \sqrt{s}=1\ {\rm TeV}, \] the predicted correction is \[ \delta_{\mathrm{sub}}(1\ {\rm TeV}) = 9.44\times10^{-2}, \] giving \[ \frac{d\sigma_{\mathrm{Synthesis}}} {d\sigma_{\mathrm{SM}}} (1\ {\rm TeV}) = 1.0944. \] The predicted relative excess is therefore \[ \frac{ d\sigma_{\mathrm{Synthesis}} - d\sigma_{\mathrm{SM}} }{ d\sigma_{\mathrm{SM}} } = 9.44\%. \] The substrate correction factor is independent of the scattering angle; the angular dependence remains contained in the Standard Model differential cross-section.
  • Observable / Test: Precision measurements of unpolarized Bhabha scattering at \[ \sqrt{s}=1\ {\rm TeV}, \] with the experimentally reconstructed ratio \[ \mathcal R_{\mathrm{Bhabha}} = \frac{ d\sigma_{\mathrm{measured}} }{ d\sigma_{\mathrm{SM}} } \] tested against the prediction \[ \mathcal R_{\mathrm{Bhabha}} = 1.0944. \]
  • Localized Falsification: Precision Bhabha-scattering measurements at \(\sqrt{s}=1\,\mathrm{TeV}\) that determine the luminosity-normalized differential cross-section ratio to be statistically incompatible with \[ \mathcal R_{\mathrm{Bhabha}} = 1.0944, \] after Standard Model radiative corrections and experimental systematic uncertainties are included, falsify this specific substrate-modified Bhabha-scattering prediction only. It does not, by itself, falsify Predictions 1 or 3.

Prediction 3: Substrate-Constrained Fermion Yukawa Coupling Ratio

  • Classification: Genuinely Novel Quantitative Fermion-Structure Prediction
  • Underlying Parameters: Vacuum Stiffness \(K=11.350\), Synthesis Pressure \(P=0.6900\), normalized unified substrate generator \(\widehat{\mathcal G}\), top-quark Yukawa coupling \(g_t\), bottom-quark Yukawa coupling \(g_b\), and the substrate equilibrium ratio \(K/(16\pi^2P)\).
  • Core Mechanism: The substrate equilibrium relation for relative Yukawa couplings is \[ \frac{g_i}{g_j} = \exp\left( \frac{K}{16\pi^2P} \right). \] For the top- and bottom-quark Yukawa couplings, \[ \frac{g_t}{g_b} = \exp\left( \frac{K}{16\pi^2P} \right). \] Using \[ K=11.350, \qquad P=0.6900, \] gives \[ \frac{K}{16\pi^2P} = 0.10430, \] and therefore \[ \frac{g_t}{g_b} = e^{0.10430} = 1.1100. \] The predicted quantity is the ratio of the Yukawa coupling parameters themselves: \[ \frac{g_t}{g_b} = 1.1100. \] The substrate therefore fixes the relative Yukawa coupling through its equilibrium structure rather than treating the two Yukawa couplings as independent parameters.
  • Observable / Test: Independent measurements of the top- and bottom-quark Higgs couplings through Higgs production and decay processes, with the experimentally reconstructed ratio \[ \mathcal R_{tb} = \frac{g_t}{g_b} \] tested against \[ \mathcal R_{tb} = 1.1100. \]
  • Localized Falsification: Precision measurements of the top- and bottom-quark Yukawa couplings that determine \(g_t/g_b\) with sufficient precision to exclude \[ \frac{g_t}{g_b} = 1.1100 \] falsify this specific substrate-constrained fermion-Yukawa-coupling prediction only. It does not, by itself, falsify Predictions 1 or 2.
