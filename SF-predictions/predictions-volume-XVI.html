Prediction 1: Substrate-Governed Critical Reynolds Threshold

  • Classification: Genuinely Novel Quantitative Fluid-Dynamic Prediction
  • Underlying Parameters: Vacuum Stiffness \(K=11.350\), reference density \(\rho_*\), reference velocity \(u_*\), background density \(\rho_b\), normalized background density \(\widehat{\rho}_b\), characteristic length \(L\), and kinematic viscosity \(\nu\).
  • Core Mechanism: The onset of turbulence occurs when the inertial stress of the fluid reaches the restorative stress capacity supplied by the recursive substrate. The substrate stress scale is \[ \mathcal K_* = K\rho_*u_*^2. \] Writing \[ \rho_b = \rho_*\widehat{\rho}_b, \] the exact threshold condition is \[ \frac12\rho_bv_{\mathrm{crit}}^2 = K\rho_*u_*^2. \] Therefore, \[ v_{\mathrm{crit}} = u_* \sqrt{ \frac{2K}{\widehat{\rho}_b} } = u_* \sqrt{ \frac{22.700}{\widehat{\rho}_b} }. \] The normalized critical velocity is \[ \widehat v_{\mathrm{crit}} = \frac{v_{\mathrm{crit}}}{u_*} = \sqrt{ \frac{22.700}{\widehat{\rho}_b} }. \] For the canonical normalized realization, \[ \widehat{\rho}_b=1, \] giving \[ \widehat v_{\mathrm{crit}} = \sqrt{22.700} = 4.764\ldots \] The Reynolds number is \[ \mathrm{Re} = \frac{vL}{\nu}, \] so the critical Reynolds number is \[ \mathrm{Re}_{\mathrm{crit}} = \sqrt{ \frac{22.700}{\widehat{\rho}_b} } \frac{u_*L}{\nu}. \] Defining the normalized Reynolds number \[ \widehat{\mathrm{Re}} := \frac{\nu}{u_*L}\mathrm{Re}, \] gives \[ \widehat{\mathrm{Re}}_{\mathrm{crit}} = \sqrt{ \frac{22.700}{\widehat{\rho}_b} }. \] For the canonical normalized density, \[ \widehat{\mathrm{Re}}_{\mathrm{crit}} = 4.764\ldots \]
  • Observable Signature: The measured critical flow velocity must satisfy \[ \frac12\rho_bv_{\mathrm{crit}}^2 = 11.350\,\rho_*u_*^2, \] or equivalently \[ \widehat v_{\mathrm{crit}} = \sqrt{ \frac{22.700}{\widehat{\rho}_b} }. \] For the canonical normalized realization, \[ \widehat v_{\mathrm{crit}} = 4.764\ldots \] and \[ \widehat{\mathrm{Re}}_{\mathrm{crit}} = 4.764\ldots \] The same threshold relation must persist systematically when the physical parameters of the flow are varied according to the derived scaling law.
  • Observable / Test: Precision controlled-flow experiments determine the transition threshold through independently measured fluid density, kinematic viscosity, characteristic length, and reference velocity. Microfluidic channels, controlled shear flows, and Taylor--Couette systems provide direct experimental geometries. For each configuration, the measured transition velocity is converted to \[ \widehat v_{\mathrm{crit}} = \frac{v_{\mathrm{crit}}}{u_*} \] and compared with \[ \sqrt{ \frac{22.700}{\widehat{\rho}_b} }. \] The corresponding Reynolds threshold is independently evaluated from \[ \mathrm{Re}_{\mathrm{crit}} = \frac{v_{\mathrm{crit}}L}{\nu} \] and compared with \[ \mathrm{Re}_{\mathrm{crit}} = \sqrt{ \frac{22.700}{\widehat{\rho}_b} } \frac{u_*L}{\nu}. \]
  • Localized Falsification: Prediction I is falsified if controlled and reproducible flow measurements establish a critical velocity that systematically violates \[ \frac12\rho_bv_{\mathrm{crit}}^2 = 11.350\,\rho_*u_*^2 \] or, equivalently, \[ \widehat v_{\mathrm{crit}} = \sqrt{ \frac{22.700}{\widehat{\rho}_b} }. \]

Prediction 2: Thermodynamic Recursive Persistence

  • Classification: Genuinely Novel Quantitative Thermodynamic Prediction
  • Underlying Parameters: The Eleventh Substrate Constant, the Self-Interaction Modulus, \[ \gamma_S = \frac{11}{12}, \] together with the recursively resolved fluctuation-energy states \(E_n\).
  • Core Mechanism: The higher-order free-energy variation is \[ \Delta F = \frac12K_{\mathrm{SM}}(\delta x)^2 + \frac{1}{4!} \chi \left( \frac{\partial^4F}{\partial x^4} \right) (\delta x)^4. \] The intrinsic recursive persistence condition is \[ \Delta E_n=0, \] which gives \[ E_{n+1} = \gamma_SE_n. \] Substitution of the derived value of \(\gamma_S\) yields the exact persistence law \[ E_{n+1} = \frac{11}{12}E_n. \] Iterating this relation gives \[ E_n = E_0 \left( \frac{11}{12} \right)^n. \] The normalized persistence observable is \[ \varepsilon_n = \frac{E_n}{E_0} = \left( \frac{11}{12} \right)^n. \] The decisive invariant is \[ R_{\mathrm{persist}} := \frac{E_{n+1}}{E_n} = \frac{11}{12} = 0.916666\ldots \] The recursive e-fold order satisfies \[ \left( \frac{11}{12} \right)^{n_e} = e^{-1}, \] giving \[ n_e = -\frac{1}{\ln(11/12)} = 11.495\ldots \] The continuous interpolation is \[ E(n) = E_0 \exp \left[ n\ln\left(\frac{11}{12}\right) \right] = E_0e^{-n/11.495\ldots}. \] The corresponding contraction exponent is \[ \lambda_S = -\ln\left(\frac{11}{12}\right) = 0.087011\ldots \] per recursive level.
  • Observable Signature: The decisive observable is the ratio of successive recursively resolved fluctuation-energy states: \[ \frac{E_1}{E_0} = \frac{11}{12}, \qquad \frac{E_2}{E_1} = \frac{11}{12}, \qquad \frac{E_3}{E_2} = \frac{11}{12}, \quad\ldots \] More generally, \[ \frac{E_{n+1}}{E_n} = \frac{11}{12}. \] The predicted sequence is therefore \[ E_0,\quad \frac{11}{12}E_0,\quad \left(\frac{11}{12}\right)^2E_0,\quad \left(\frac{11}{12}\right)^3E_0,\quad\ldots \] This recursive contraction ratio constitutes the parameter-free observable signature of the thermodynamic persistence law.
  • Observable / Test: High-resolution measurements of fluctuation energy in thermodynamic systems with independently resolvable successive recursive fluctuation states provide the direct test. Suitable systems include controlled fluid, condensed-matter, and other thermodynamic systems exhibiting hierarchical relaxation or critical fluctuation dynamics. For an experimentally resolved sequence, \[ E_0,E_1,E_2,\ldots, \] the successive ratios \[ R_n = \frac{E_{n+1}}{E_n} \] are determined and compared with \[ R_n = \frac{11}{12}. \] The complete measured sequence is additionally tested against \[ E_n = E_0 \left( \frac{11}{12} \right)^n. \]
  • Localized Falsification: Prediction II is falsified if controlled and reproducible measurement of successive recursively resolved fluctuation-energy states establishes a systematic contraction ratio incompatible with \[ \frac{E_{n+1}}{E_n} = \frac{11}{12}. \] Equivalently, the prediction is falsified if the measured sequence cannot satisfy \[ E_n = E_0 \left( \frac{11}{12} \right)^n \] over the recursively resolved regime governed by intrinsic substrate persistence.
