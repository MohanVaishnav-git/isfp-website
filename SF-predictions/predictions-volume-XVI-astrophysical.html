Prediction 1: Intrinsic Axial Rotation of the Galactic Core

  • Classification: Genuinely Novel Astrophysical Prediction
  • Underlying Parameters: Galactic-Core angular momentum \(\mathbf{J}_{\rm core}\), intrinsic rotational state of the Galactic Core, and the measured three-dimensional motions of Galactic-Centre stellar systems.
  • Core Mechanism: The Galactic Core possesses an intrinsic axial rotation distinct from the orbital motion of the surrounding stellar systems: \[ \text{Galactic Core} \longrightarrow \text{Intrinsic Axial Rotation}. \] The predicted rotational state is an intrinsic dynamical property of the central Galactic Core rather than a consequence inferred solely from the orbital motion of surrounding stars. The core rotational state therefore constitutes an independent dynamical degree of freedom of the Galactic Core.
  • Observable Signature: The predicted observable is a persistent intrinsic rotational axis and corresponding angular-momentum vector of the Galactic Core. The rotational state produces a coherent rotational signature in the central region together with associated relativistic effects on the motion of closely orbiting stellar systems. In particular, a rotating central core produces a measurable rotational orientation and corresponding dynamical influence on sufficiently close orbital trajectories.
  • Observable / Test: High-resolution, time-resolved observations of the Galactic Centre through infrared, millimetre, and radio observations, together with precision astrometry and long-term monitoring of closely orbiting stars. The rotational state is tested through the detection of a persistent rotational axis, angular-momentum vector, rotational signature, and associated relativistic orbital effects. Precision measurements of orbital-plane precession provide a direct dynamical test of the central rotational state.
  • Localized Falsification: Failure of sufficiently precise and sufficiently long-duration observations to detect any intrinsic axial rotation of the Galactic Core falsifies this specific Galactic-Core rotation prediction only. It does not, by itself, falsify Predictions 2, 3, or 4.

Prediction 2: Galactic-Core Rotation and Peripheral Orbital Organization

  • Classification: Genuinely Novel Astrophysical Prediction
  • Underlying Parameters: Galactic-Core angular-momentum vector \(\mathbf{J}_{\rm core}\) and the orbital angular-momentum vectors \(\mathbf{L}_i\) of the surrounding stellar systems.
  • Core Mechanism: The intrinsic axial rotation of the Galactic Core participates in organizing the angular-momentum structure and orbital motion of the stellar periphery: \[ \mathbf{J}_{\rm core} \longrightarrow \{\mathbf{L}_1,\mathbf{L}_2,\mathbf{L}_3,\ldots\}. \] Equivalently, \[ \text{Galactic-Core Axial Rotation} \longrightarrow \text{Galactic Angular-Momentum Organization} \longrightarrow \text{Stellar Orbital Motion}. \] Prediction 2 therefore concerns the dynamical relationship between the intrinsic rotational state of the Galactic Core and the orbital angular-momentum distribution of its surrounding stellar systems.
  • Observable Signature: A statistically significant and persistent correlation between the Galactic-Core angular-momentum vector \(\mathbf{J}_{\rm core}\) and the orbital angular-momentum vectors \(\mathbf{L}_i\) of surrounding stellar systems. The predicted organization extends to orbital-plane orientations, orbital inclinations, and their secular evolution. The observable quantity is therefore the measured angular-momentum distribution \[ \{\mathbf{L}_1,\mathbf{L}_2,\mathbf{L}_3,\ldots\} \] relative to \[ \mathbf{J}_{\rm core}. \]
  • Observable / Test: Precision measurement of stellar positions, proper motions, radial velocities, orbital inclinations, orbital-plane orientations, orbital angular-momentum vectors, and secular changes in orbital orientation. The transverse proper motion and line-of-sight radial velocity determine the three-dimensional stellar velocity, from which \[ \mathbf{L}_i = \mathbf{r}_i \times \mathbf{p}_i \] is determined. The resulting angular-momentum distribution is tested for a statistically significant and persistent correlation with \(\mathbf{J}_{\rm core}\).
  • Localized Falsification: If intrinsic Galactic-Core rotation is established but no statistically significant and persistent relationship is found between the core's angular-momentum vector and the orbital angular-momentum distribution of the surrounding stellar systems, this specific core--periphery angular-momentum prediction is falsified. It does not, by itself, falsify Predictions 1, 3, or 4.

Prediction 3: The Galactic Electroweak Core

  • Classification: Genuinely Novel Quantitative Astrophysical Prediction
  • Underlying Parameters: Electroweak critical substrate ratio \[ \left(\frac{P}{K}\right)_{\rm EW} \approx 1.6, \] vacuum stiffness \(K\), synthesis pressure \(P\), the substrate Lagrangian \(\mathcal L_{\rm sub}\), the Synthesis Tensor \(\Sigma_{\mu\nu}\), the spacetime field \(G_{\mu\nu}\), and the galactic acceleration scale \(a_0\).
  • Core Mechanism: The Galactic Core is an active Electroweak field nexus occupying the critical Electroweak synthesis regime: \[ \left(\frac{P}{K}\right)_{\rm Galactic\,Core} \approx 1.6. \] At this critical ratio, the substrate enters the Electroweak high-symmetry state and develops extreme nonlinear field concentration. The complete field-theoretic chain is \[ \begin{aligned} K,P &\longrightarrow \frac{P}{K} \longrightarrow \mathcal L_{\rm sub} \longrightarrow \Sigma_{\mu\nu} \longrightarrow G_{\mu\nu}\\ &\longrightarrow \text{Galactic Gravitational Field} \longrightarrow \text{Galactic-Core Dynamics}. \end{aligned} \] The critical Galactic-Core regime therefore produces the successive physical states \[ \begin{aligned} \frac{P}{K}\approx1.6 &\longrightarrow \text{Electroweak Symmetry} \longrightarrow \text{Extreme Nonlinearity}\\ &\longrightarrow \text{Energetic Core} \longrightarrow \text{Extreme Gravitation}. \end{aligned} \] The same substrate field formulation generates the galactic peripheral gravitational response through its weak-field limit: \[ a_{\rm tot} = a_N+\sqrt{a_Na_0}. \] The complete field-to-observable chain is therefore \[ (K,P) \longrightarrow \frac{P}{K} \longrightarrow \Sigma_{\mu\nu} \longrightarrow G_{\mu\nu} \longrightarrow a_K \longrightarrow a_{\rm tot}. \] The extreme Galactic-Core state and the peripheral structural acceleration are therefore two dynamical regimes of the same substrate field formulation. The Galactic Core is consequently predicted to be an active Electroweak substrate concentration rather than a passive terminal mass-concentration state.
  • Observable Signature: The predicted Galactic Core exhibits an extreme energetic and gravitational state corresponding to the critical Electroweak synthesis regime \[ \left(\frac{P}{K}\right)_{\rm EW} \approx 1.6. \] The central state is characterized by extreme nonlinear field concentration, energetic activity, relativistic gravitational effects, and associated plasma and outflow phenomena. At larger galactic radii, the same substrate field produces the structural acceleration \[ a_K = \sqrt{a_Na_0}, \] and hence \[ a_{\rm tot} = a_N+\sqrt{a_Na_0}. \] The central energetic regime and peripheral acceleration regime therefore constitute two observable manifestations of the same substrate field structure.
  • Observable / Test: Determination of the physical state of the Galactic Core through high-resolution observations of its central energy concentration, electromagnetic and relativistic activity, plasma environment, energetic outflows, jet production, and gravitational properties. The observed central gravitational and energetic state is tested against the predicted critical Electroweak substrate condition \[ \left(\frac{P}{K}\right)_{\rm EW} \approx 1.6 \] and its associated nonlinear field response. The corresponding peripheral gravitational field is independently tested against the substrate-derived acceleration relation \[ a_{\rm tot} = a_N+\sqrt{a_Na_0}. \] The combined test therefore examines both the critical central state and its predicted connection to the galactic-scale gravitational field.
  • Localized Falsification: Observation of a Galactic-Core physical state incompatible with the critical Electroweak synthesis regime \[ \left(\frac{P}{K}\right)_{\rm EW} \approx 1.6, \] or demonstration that the central gravitational state is incompatible with the predicted nonlinear substrate field response, falsifies this specific Galactic Electroweak Core prediction only. It does not, by itself, falsify Predictions 1, 2, or 4.

Prediction 4: The Cosmic Core as the Master Symmetry--GUT Hierarchy

  • Classification: Genuinely Novel Quantitative Astrophysical Prediction
  • Underlying Parameters: The Grand-Unification substrate ratio \[ \left(\frac{P}{K}\right)_{\rm GUT} \approx 1.45\times10^{14} \] and the Master-Symmetry substrate ratio \[ \left(\frac{P}{K}\right)_{\rm MS} \approx 1.47\times10^{18}. \]
  • Core Mechanism: The Cosmic Core contains a hierarchical high-order symmetry structure in which the Master Symmetry occupies the higher-order region and the Grand-Unification structure occupies the corresponding lower-order region: \[ \text{Cosmic Core} \supset \text{Master Symmetry} \supset \text{GUT Structure}. \] The corresponding substrate hierarchy is \[ \left(\frac{P}{K}\right)_{\rm GUT} \approx 1.45\times10^{14} \longrightarrow \left(\frac{P}{K}\right)_{\rm MS} \approx 1.47\times10^{18}. \] The resulting structural ordering extends outward as \[ \begin{aligned} \text{Master Symmetry} &\longrightarrow \text{GUT Structure} \longrightarrow \text{Electroweak Structure}\\ &\longrightarrow \text{Differentiated Matter} \longrightarrow \text{Peripheral Structure}. \end{aligned} \] The Cosmic Core is therefore predicted to be a permanent hierarchical high-order symmetry domain rather than a vanished chronological state. The Master Symmetry and GUT states remain successive structural levels of the Cosmic Core while lower-order states differentiate outward through the cosmic hierarchy.
  • Observable Signature: A persistent Cosmic Core possessing a hierarchical high-order symmetry organization in which the Master-Symmetry and Grand-Unification states occupy distinct levels of the same cosmic structure. The predicted hierarchy is characterized quantitatively by \[ \left(\frac{P}{K}\right)_{\rm GUT} \approx 1.45\times10^{14} \] and \[ \left(\frac{P}{K}\right)_{\rm MS} \approx 1.47\times10^{18}. \] The corresponding structural hierarchy is \[ \begin{aligned} \text{Cosmic Master Symmetry} &\longrightarrow \text{Cosmic GUT Structure} \longrightarrow \text{Galactic Electroweak Cores}\\ &\longrightarrow \text{Stellar Energetic Cores} \longrightarrow \text{Planetary Systems}. \end{aligned} \]
  • Observable / Test: Identification of a persistent Cosmic Core and determination of whether large-scale cosmic organization exhibits the predicted hierarchical relationship between the Master-Symmetry and GUT states, together with observational signatures of the corresponding higher-order organization of galactic systems. The quantitative test targets \[ \left(\frac{P}{K}\right)_{\rm GUT} \approx 1.45\times10^{14} \] and \[ \left(\frac{P}{K}\right)_{\rm MS} \approx 1.47\times10^{18}. \] The cosmic-scale kinematic test additionally examines whether sufficiently precise three-dimensional galaxy velocities reveal a coherent orbital component organized around a common cosmic centre: \[ \mathbf v_{\rm gal} = \mathbf v_{\perp} + \mathbf v_{\parallel}. \] A coherent large-scale orbital organization around a common centre constitutes the direct dynamical test of the predicted Cosmic Core.
  • Localized Falsification: Failure to establish a persistent Cosmic Core possessing the predicted Master-Symmetry--GUT hierarchy, absence of the corresponding high-order symmetry states, disagreement with either predicted substrate ratio \[ \left(\frac{P}{K}\right)_{\rm GUT} \approx 1.45\times10^{14} \] or \[ \left(\frac{P}{K}\right)_{\rm MS} \approx 1.47\times10^{18}, \] or demonstrated incompatibility between the observed large-scale cosmic organization and the predicted hierarchy falsifies this specific Cosmic Master-Symmetry--GUT prediction only. It does not, by itself, falsify Predictions 1, 2, or 3.
