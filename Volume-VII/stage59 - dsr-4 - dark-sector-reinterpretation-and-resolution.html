$K = |\nabla C|, \qquad P = P_{\text{syn}}$

$\rho_{\text{eff}}(x) = \frac{K^2(x)}{P(x)}$

Thus:

$\rho_{\text{total}} \rightarrow \rho_{\text{eff}}$

Dark energy is not independent but arises from synthesis pressure:

$\Lambda \sim P$

Thus:

$\Lambda \rightarrow P$

Cosmic expansion becomes:

$H^2 = \frac{8\pi G}{3} \frac{K^2}{P} + \frac{P}{3}$

Thus:

$H^2 = \mathcal{F}(K, P)$

5. Unified Gravitational Potential

Gravitational field equation:

$\nabla^2 \Phi = 4\pi G \frac{K^2}{P}$

Thus, Poisson equation becomes substrate-dependent.

6. Rotation Curve Equation

Galaxy dynamics:

$v^2(r) = \frac{G}{r} \int_0^r \frac{K^2(r')}{P(r')} 4\pi r'^2 dr'$

Flat curves condition:

$\frac{K^2(r)}{P(r)} \sim \frac{1}{r^2}$

7. Gravitational Lensing Equation

Light deflection:

$\alpha = \frac{4G}{c^2} \int \frac{K^2(x')}{P(x')} \frac{1}{|x - x'|} d^3x'$

Thus:

$\alpha = \mathcal{A}(K, P)$

$\frac{\ddot{a}}{a} = P - \frac{4\pi G}{3} \frac{K^2}{P}$

Thus:

$\text{Acceleration} = \mathcal{G}(K, P)$

9. Unified Observable Mapping

All observables:

$\mathcal{O} = \mathcal{F}(K, P)$

No dependence on independent dark sector variables.

10. Structural Elimination

Standard variables eliminated:

$(\rho_{\text{DM}}, \Lambda) \rightarrow \emptyset$

$\text{Dark Sector} = \text{Effective Description of } (K, P)$

$\frac{\partial \mathcal{O}}{\partial X_i} = 0 \quad \forall X_i \notin (K, P)$

$\frac{\partial \mathcal{O}}{\partial \rho_{\text{DM}}} = 0, \quad \frac{\partial \mathcal{O}}{\partial \Lambda} = 0$

$n_{\text{substrate}} = 2 \quad (K, P)$

$n_{\text{standard}} = 3 \quad (\rho_{\text{visible}}, \rho_{\text{DM}}, \Lambda)$

$\rho_{\text{eff}} = \frac{K^2}{P}, \qquad \Lambda = P$
