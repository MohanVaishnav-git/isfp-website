Stage 47 — Mathematical Structure

Particles as Quantized On-Shell Field Excitations

🔷 1. FIELD FOUNDATION

A relativistic field \phi(x) satisfies: $$(\Box + m^2)\,\phi(x) = 0$$ with plane-wave solutions: $$\phi(x) \sim e^{-ip \cdot x}, \quad p^2 = m^2$$

🔷 2. MODE DECOMPOSITION

The field admits a Fourier expansion: $$\phi(x,t) = \int \frac{d^3 k}{(2\pi)^3} \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\omega_k}} \left( a_{\mathbf{k}} e^{-ik\cdot x} + a_{\mathbf{k}}^\dagger e^{ik\cdot x} \right)$$ $$\omega_k = \sqrt{\mathbf{k}^2 + m^2}$$

🔷 3. QUANTIZATION

Operators satisfy: $$[a_{\mathbf{k}}, a_{\mathbf{k}'}^\dagger] = (2\pi)^3 \delta^3(\mathbf{k}-\mathbf{k}')$$ Hamiltonian: $$H = \int \frac{d^3 k}{(2\pi)^3} \, \omega_k \left(a_{\mathbf{k}}^\dagger a_{\mathbf{k}} + \tfrac{1}{2}\right)$$

🔷 4. PARTICLE STATES

Vacuum: $$a_{\mathbf{k}} |0\rangle = 0$$ Single-particle state: $$|1_{\mathbf{k}}\rangle = a_{\mathbf{k}}^\dagger |0\rangle$$ General: $$|n_{\mathbf{k}}\rangle = \frac{(a_{\mathbf{k}}^\dagger)^n}{\sqrt{n!}} |0\rangle$$

🔷 5. ENERGY SPECTRUM

$$E_{n_{\mathbf{k}}} = \left(n_{\mathbf{k}} + \tfrac{1}{2}\right)\omega_k$$

🔷 6. IDENTITY FROM SYMMETRY

Field transforms under group G: $$\phi \rightarrow U(g)\phi$$ Generators T^a define charges: $$Q^a |\psi\rangle = q^a |\psi\rangle$$ Spin: $$\mathbf{J}^2 |\psi\rangle = s(s+1)|\psi\rangle$$

🔷 7. PARTICLE IDENTITY

$$\boxed{ \text{Particle} = (m,\; s,\; \{q^a\},\; R) }$$

🔷 8. ON-SHELL CONDITION

$$p^2 = m^2 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad E^2 = p^2 + m^2$$

🔷 9. PROPAGATOR CRITERION

$$\Delta(p) = \frac{1}{p^2 - m^2 + i\epsilon}$$ $$p^2 = m^2 \;\Rightarrow\; \text{pole (physical particle)}$$

🔷 10. REALITY SELECTION

$$p^2 = m^2 \;\Rightarrow\; \text{on-shell (observable)}$$ $$p^2 \neq m^2 \;\Rightarrow\; \text{off-shell (virtual)}$$

🔷 🔶 COMPLETION THEOREM (STAGE 47)

$$\boxed{ \text{A particle is a quantized, on-shell excitation of a field mode,} }$$

$$\boxed{ \text{whose identity is determined by symmetry representations of the field.} }$$

🔷 11. FULL STRUCTURAL CHAIN

$$\boxed{ \text{Field} \;\Rightarrow\; \text{Modes} \;\Rightarrow\; \text{Quantization} \;\Rightarrow\; \text{Identity} \;\Rightarrow\; \text{On-Shell Reality} }$$

🔷 12. FINAL ONE-LINE STATEMENT

$$\boxed{ \text{Particles are on-shell quanta of fields with symmetry-defined identity.} }$$

Mathematical Summary

Structural Identity:
$$\hat{\Phi} \rightarrow |n, q, m, s\rangle_{on-shell}$$

Stage 47 mathematically unifies the field-to-particle transition by defining physical reality as the intersection of quantization, symmetry representation, and the on-shell invariant.
