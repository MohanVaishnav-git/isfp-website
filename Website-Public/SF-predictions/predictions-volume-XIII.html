Status: Sub-Threshold Saturation Drag and Pressure-Modulated Mass-Energy Dispersion — Two Predictions

Prediction 1: Non-Linear Sub-Threshold Saturation Drag

  • Classification: Genuinely Novel Quantitative Relativistic Kinematic Prediction
  • Underlying Parameters: Vacuum Stiffness \(K=11.350\), Synthesis Pressure \(P=0.6900\), recursive simultaneity invariant \(\gamma_S=11/12\), substrate propagation speed \(c_S\), substrate reference density \(\rho_0\), particle velocity \(v\), and substrate Lorentz factor \(\gamma_{\mathrm{sub}}\).
  • Core Mechanism: The finite substrate saturation capacity, together with the kinetic displacement flux and pressure-dependent restorative response, produces a non-linear correction to the standard Lorentz scaling. The exact closed substrate Lorentz factor is \[ \gamma_{\mathrm{sub}} = \left[ 1-\frac{v^2}{c_S^2} -\frac{P}{K\rho_0} \frac{v^4}{c_S^4} \right]^{-1/2}. \] Equivalently, \[ \gamma_{\mathrm{sub}} = \left[ 1- \frac{v^2}{c_S^2} \left( 1+ \frac{v^2}{c_S^2} \frac{P}{K\rho_0} \right) \right]^{-1/2}. \] The standard special-relativistic value is \[ \gamma_{\mathrm{SR}} = \left(1-\frac{v^2}{c_S^2}\right)^{-1/2}. \] The leading fractional deviation is \[ \frac{ \gamma_{\mathrm{sub}}-\gamma_{\mathrm{SR}} }{ \gamma_{\mathrm{SR}} } = \frac{1}{2} \frac{P}{K\rho_0} \frac{(v/c_S)^4} {1-v^2/c_S^2} + O\!\left( \left(\frac{P}{K\rho_0}\right)^2 \right). \] Using \[ K=11.350, \qquad P=0.6900, \] the closed numerical relation is \[ \gamma_{\mathrm{sub}} = \left[ 1- \frac{v^2}{c_S^2} - \frac{0.6900}{11.350\,\rho_0} \frac{v^4}{c_S^4} \right]^{-1/2}. \] The characteristic correction therefore follows the fourth-power velocity dependence \[ \Delta\gamma_{\mathrm{sub}} \propto \left(\frac{v}{c_S}\right)^4, \] with the relative effect increasing toward the substrate saturation limit.
  • Observable Signature: The predicted phenomenon is a non-linear modification of ultra-relativistic kinematic scaling as the kinetic displacement flux approaches the finite substrate saturation capacity. The principal signatures are a reproducible deviation of ultra-relativistic particle energy-velocity relations from standard Lorentz scaling, an enhanced effective resistance to further kinetic displacement as \(v\) approaches \(c_S\), a fractional deviation following the characteristic \[ \left(\frac{v}{c_S}\right)^4 \] dependence, and systematic dependence of the deviation on the substrate synthesis pressure \(P\).
  • Observable / Test: High-precision measurements of ultra-relativistic particle kinematics under calibrated substrate-pressure conditions, testing the departure from \[ \gamma_{\mathrm{SR}} = \left(1-\frac{v^2}{c_S^2}\right)^{-1/2} \] against the predicted relation \[ \frac{ \gamma_{\mathrm{sub}}-\gamma_{\mathrm{SR}} }{ \gamma_{\mathrm{SR}} } = \frac{1}{2} \frac{P}{K\rho_0} \frac{(v/c_S)^4} {1-v^2/c_S^2} + O\!\left( \left(\frac{P}{K\rho_0}\right)^2 \right). \]
  • Localized Falsification: High-precision measurements of ultra-relativistic particle kinematics under calibrated substrate-pressure conditions that fail to exhibit the closed relation \[ \gamma_{\mathrm{sub}} = \left[ 1-\frac{v^2}{c_S^2} -\frac{P}{K\rho_0} \frac{v^4}{c_S^4} \right]^{-1/2} \] falsify this specific sub-threshold saturation-drag prediction only. It does not, by itself, falsify Prediction 2.

Prediction 2: Pressure-Modulated Mass-Energy Dispersion

  • Classification: Genuinely Novel Quantitative Energetic Prediction
  • Underlying Parameters: Vacuum Stiffness \(K=11.350\), Synthesis Pressure \(P\), substrate inertial density \(\Psi(P)\), substrate propagation speed \(c_S\), substrate mass \(M_S\), substrate rest energy \(E_S(P)\), reference pressure \(P_0\), and reference inertial density \(\Psi_0=\Psi(P_0)\).
  • Core Mechanism: The first-principles substrate propagation speed is \[ c_S^2 = \frac{K}{\Psi(P)}. \] The substrate mass-energy equivalence is \[ E_S=M_Sc_S^2. \] Combining these relations gives the exact pressure-dependent substrate rest-energy relation \[ E_S(P) = M_S\frac{K}{\Psi(P)}. \] For a reference pressure \(P_0\), \[ \Psi_0=\Psi(P_0), \qquad E_{S,0} = M_S\frac{K}{\Psi_0}. \] The exact energy ratio is therefore \[ \frac{E_S(P)}{E_{S,0}} = \frac{\Psi_0}{\Psi(P)}. \] The logarithmic pressure response is \[ \frac{1}{E_S} \frac{dE_S}{dP} = -\frac{\Psi'(P)}{\Psi(P)}. \] For \[ P=P_0+\Delta P, \] the leading-order fractional energy modulation is \[ \frac{\Delta E_S}{E_{S,0}} = - \left. \frac{\Psi'(P)} {\Psi(P)} \right|_{P_0} \Delta P + O(\Delta P^2). \] Using \[ K=11.350, \] the complete energy relation is \[ E_S(P) = M_S\frac{11.350}{\Psi(P)}. \] The magnitude and sign of the pressure response are fixed by the first-principles pressure dependence of the substrate inertial density \(\Psi(P)\).
  • Observable Signature: The predicted phenomenon is a deterministic pressure-induced variation of the rest-energy equivalence of a fixed stored equilibrium. The principal signatures are a pressure-correlated shift in precision nuclear transition energies under controlled high-pressure conditions, a corresponding pressure-dependent shift in atomic or condensed-matter spectral lines after thermal and gravitational contributions are removed, and a reproducible fractional energy shift obeying \[ \frac{\Delta E_S}{E_{S,0}} = - \left. \frac{\Psi'(P)} {\Psi(P)} \right|_{P_0} \Delta P \] to leading order.
  • Observable / Test: Precision spectroscopy over a controlled pressure interval \(\Delta P\), including precision nuclear transition measurements and atomic or condensed-matter spectral measurements, with thermal and gravitational contributions independently controlled or removed. The measured energy shift is tested against \[ \frac{E_S(P)}{E_{S,0}} = \frac{\Psi_0}{\Psi(P)} \] and, for small pressure variations, \[ \frac{\Delta E_S}{E_{S,0}} = - \left. \frac{\Psi'(P)} {\Psi(P)} \right|_{P_0} \Delta P. \]
  • Localized Falsification: Precision spectroscopy performed over a controlled pressure interval \(\Delta P\) that fails to exhibit the quantitatively predicted relation \[ \frac{\Delta E_S}{E_{S,0}} = - \left. \frac{\Psi'(P)} {\Psi(P)} \right|_{P_0} \Delta P \] falsifies this specific pressure-modulated mass-energy dispersion prediction only. It does not, by itself, falsify Prediction 1.
