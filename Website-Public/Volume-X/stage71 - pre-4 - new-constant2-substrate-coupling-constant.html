Module 6: Rigorous Synthesis of $\kappa_S$

Stage 71 provides the first-principles derivation of the parent coupling constant ($\kappa_S$). While standard physics relies on empirically measured coupling strengths, the Synthesis Framework derives gauge efficiency directly from the geometric interaction of the substrate fields.

1. THE INTERACTION TENSION ($T_0$)

The baseline linear tension is the geometric balance between the conserving field strength ($K$) and the motive pressure ($P$):

$T_0 = \sqrt{K \cdot P} = \sqrt{11.350 \cdot 0.6900} \approx 2.79848$

2. MANIFOLD IMPEDANCE ($Z$)

Resistance to triadic flow is a function of the $S^3$ geometry's volumetric cross-section:

$Z = \frac{3\pi}{2} \approx 4.71239$

3. THE COUPLING IDENTITY

Total substrate stress equipartitioned across the three functional degrees of freedom:

$\boxed{\kappa_S = \frac{\sqrt{K \cdot P}}{3 \cdot Z} \approx 0.19795}$

4. OBSERVABLE CORRESPONDENCE

This constant acts as the vacuum baseline for the Weak Mixing Angle. Any shift to the standard value ($\approx 0.23$) is interpreted as material loading upon the pure substrate geometry.
