Summary: Mathematical Structure of Stage 36

1. Coupled Particle–Detector Dynamics

The measurement process begins when the particle excitation couples to the detector substrate through an interaction Hamiltonian:

$H = H_{p} + H_{D} + H_{int}$

where $H_{int} = A\,V(x,X)$. The combined system state evolves according to:

$i\hbar \frac{\partial \Psi(x,X,t)}{\partial t} = \left(H_{p}+H_{D}+H_{int}\right)\Psi(x,X,t)$

This establishes the coupled particle–detector system.

2. Resonant Mode Matching

Efficient interaction occurs when the spectral modes of the particle and detector overlap.

  • Particle spectral representation: $\psi(\omega)$
  • Detector response spectrum: $D(\omega)$
  • Resonance condition: $\omega_p \approx \omega_D$

Spectral overlap integral:

$R=\int \psi(\omega)\,D(\omega)\,d\omega$

Large $R$ produces strong energy transfer between particle and detector.

3. Amplification Cascade

The microscopic detector excitation evolves into macroscopic signal through exponential amplification of detector modes.

Detector excitation: $\phi(x,t)=\sum_i a_i(t)\chi_i(x)$

Mode growth equation: $\frac{da_i}{dt}=k_i a_i$

Solution: $a_i(t)=a_i(0)e^{k_i t}$

Total detector signal:

$S(t)=\sum_i a_i(0)e^{k_i t}$

The cascade occurs when the gain exceeds damping: $k_i > \gamma$.

4. Decoherence Dynamics

Environmental interaction produces entanglement between detector and environment:

$|\Psi\rangle = \sum_i c_i |s_i\rangle |D_i\rangle |E_i\rangle$

Reduced density matrix after tracing out environment:

$\rho = \sum_{i,j} c_i c_j^* \langle E_j|E_i\rangle |s_i\rangle |D_i\rangle \langle s_j| \langle D_j|$

Environmental orthogonality: $\langle E_j|E_i\rangle \rightarrow \delta_{ij}$. Therefore, $\rho_{ij} \rightarrow 0$. The system becomes a classical probability mixture.

Decoherence decay law: $\rho_{ij}(t)=\rho_{ij}(0)e^{-t/\tau_D}$.

5. Outcome Channel Formation

After decoherence the detector state decomposes into stable pointer states $\{|d_1\rangle, |d_2\rangle, ... , |d_n\rangle\}$. Density matrix becomes:

$\rho = \sum_i |c_i|^2 |s_i\rangle |d_i\rangle \langle s_i| \langle d_i|$

Projection operators defining channels: $P_i = |d_i\rangle\langle d_i|$. Channel decomposition:

$|\Psi\rangle = \sum_i P_i |\Psi\rangle$

Each component $|\Psi_i\rangle = P_i |\Psi\rangle$ represents a distinct outcome channel.

6. Complete Mathematical Flow of Stage 36

The full dynamical sequence of the pre-measurement process is:

$\Psi_p \rightarrow H_{int} \rightarrow R \rightarrow S(t) \rightarrow \rho_{ij} \rightarrow 0 \rightarrow P_i$

  1. Particle excitation
  2. Interaction with detector
  3. Resonant spectral overlap
  4. Amplification cascade
  5. Environmental decoherence
  6. Formation of outcome channels

Stage 36 therefore ends with the formation of dynamically stable outcome channels in the detector substrate. These channels represent the physically available future measurement states from which a single outcome will emerge in the next stage.
