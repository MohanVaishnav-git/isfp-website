STAGE 52 — MATHEMATICAL STRUCTURE SUMMARY

Gauge Bosons (Interaction Carriers)

(Symmetry → Connection → Quantization → Interaction → Closure)

1. FOUNDATIONAL STRUCTURE

Gauge symmetry:

$$G = U(1)\times SU(2)\times SU(3)$$

$$\dim(G) = 1 + 3 + 8 = 12$$

Generators:

$$[T^a, T^b] = i f^{abc} T^c$$

2. GAUGE CONNECTION (INFRASTRUCTURE)

$$A_\mu = A_\mu^a T^a$$

Covariant derivative:

$$D_\mu = \partial_\mu + i g A_\mu^a T^a$$

3. FIELD DYNAMICS (CURVATURE)

$$F_{\mu\nu}^a = \partial_\mu A_\nu^a - \partial_\nu A_\mu^a + g f^{abc} A_\mu^b A_\nu^c$$

Gauge field Lagrangian:

$$\mathcal{L}_{gauge} = -\frac{1}{4} F_{\mu\nu}^a F^{\mu\nu a}$$

4. QUANTIZATION OF GAUGE FIELD

$$A_\mu^a(x) \rightarrow \hat{A}_\mu^a(x)$$

Mode expansion:

$$\hat{A}_\mu^a = \sum_{\mathbf{k},\lambda} \left( a^a_{\mathbf{k},\lambda} \epsilon_\mu e^{-ikx} + a^{a\dagger}_{\mathbf{k},\lambda} \epsilon_\mu^* e^{ikx} \right)$$

5. BOSON EMERGENCE

$$a^{a\dagger}|0\rangle = |1^a\rangle$$

$$\text{Gauge boson} = \text{quantized excitation of } A_\mu^a$$

6. GENERATOR → BOSON MAPPING

$$T^a \;\Rightarrow\; A_\mu^a \;\Rightarrow\; \text{Gauge Boson}_a$$

$$\dim(G) = N \Rightarrow N \text{ gauge bosons}$$

$$1 + 3 + 8 = 12$$

7. INTERACTION STRUCTURE

From covariant derivative:

$$\mathcal{L}_{int} = g \bar{\psi}\gamma^\mu T^a \psi A_\mu^a$$

Define current:

$$J^\mu_a = \bar{\psi}\gamma^\mu T^a \psi$$

Thus:

$$\mathcal{L}_{int} = g J^\mu_a A_\mu^a$$

8. EXCHANGE MECHANISM

Scattering amplitude:

$$\mathcal{M} = J^\mu_a \; D_{\mu\nu}^{ab}(k) \; J^\nu_b$$

Propagator:

$$D_{\mu\nu}^{ab}(k) = \frac{-i \delta^{ab} g_{\mu\nu}}{k^2 + i\epsilon}$$

9. COMPLETE INTERACTION LAGRANGIAN

$$\boxed{ \mathcal{L} = \bar{\psi}(i\gamma^\mu D_\mu - m)\psi -\frac{1}{4}F_{\mu\nu}^a F^{\mu\nu a} }$$

10. FIELD EQUATIONS

Dirac equation:

$$(i\gamma^\mu D_\mu - m)\psi = 0$$

Yang–Mills equation:

$$D^\mu F_{\mu\nu}^a = g J_\nu^a$$

11. CONSERVATION LAW

$$D_\mu J^\mu_a = 0$$

12. STRUCTURAL CHAIN

$$G \;\Rightarrow\; T^a \;\Rightarrow\; A_\mu^a \;\Rightarrow\; \hat{A}_\mu^a \;\Rightarrow\; a^\dagger \;\Rightarrow\; \text{Bosons} \;\Rightarrow\; J^\mu A_\mu \;\Rightarrow\; \mathcal{L}$$

13. NECESSITY STATEMENT

$$\text{Local symmetry} \;\Rightarrow\; \text{Gauge connection} \;\Rightarrow\; \text{Quantization} \;\Rightarrow\; \text{Bosons} \;\Rightarrow\; \text{Interaction}$$

14. FINAL STRUCTURAL RESULT

$$\boxed{ \text{Gauge symmetry fully determines interaction carriers and interaction structure} }$$

15. CONCEPTUAL COMPRESSION

$$\boxed{ \text{Symmetry} \;\rightarrow\; \text{Connection} \;\rightarrow\; \text{Quanta} \;\rightarrow\; \text{Interaction} \;\rightarrow\; \text{Closure} }$$

16. CLOSURE STATEMENT (STAGE 52)

$$\boxed{ \text{Interactions are not introduced; they are inevitable consequences of symmetry quantization} }$$
