Stage 53 — Mathematical Structure & Closure Theorem

🔹 1. Scalar Field and Potential

Scalar potential: $$V(\phi) = \mu^2 |\phi|^2 + \lambda |\phi|^4$$

Stability condition: $$\lambda > 0$$

Symmetry breaking condition: $$\mu^2 < 0$$

🔹 2. Vacuum Structure (Degeneracy)

Minimization condition: $$\frac{\partial V}{\partial \phi} = 0 \Rightarrow |\phi| = v = \sqrt{\frac{-\mu^2}{\lambda}}$$

Vacuum manifold: $$\Omega = \{ \phi : |\phi| = v \}$$

🔹 3. Instability of Symmetric State

Second derivative: $$\frac{\partial^2 V}{\partial \phi^2}\Big|_{\phi=0} = \mu^2 < 0$$

Therefore: $$\phi = 0 \text{ is unstable}$$

🔹 4. Vacuum Selection (VEV)

Vacuum expectation value: $$\langle \phi \rangle = v$$

Field expansion: $$\phi(x) = v + h(x)$$

🔹 5. Mass Generation (Fermions — Yukawa)

Interaction Lagrangian: $$\mathcal{L}_{Yukawa} = -g \bar{\psi} \phi \psi$$

After substitution: $$m = g v$$

🔹 6. Gauge Boson Masses

$$m_W = \frac{1}{2} g v$$

$$m_Z = \frac{1}{2} \sqrt{g^2 + g'^2}\, v$$

🔹 7. Complete Mass Spectrum

Mass relation: $$m_i = g_i v$$

Mass ratios: $$\frac{m_i}{m_j} = \frac{g_i}{g_j}$$

🔹 8. Closure Theorem

$$\boxed{ \text{Given } \mu^2 < 0,\; \text{the scalar field develops } \langle \phi \rangle = v,\; \text{and particle masses arise as } m_i = g_i v }$$

🔹 9. Full Structural Chain

$$\boxed{ \text{Symmetric Lagrangian} \rightarrow \text{Degenerate Vacuum} \rightarrow \text{Instability at } \phi = 0 \rightarrow \langle \phi \rangle = v }$$

$$\boxed{ \rightarrow \text{Coupling to Vacuum} \rightarrow m_i = g_i v \rightarrow \text{Mass Spectrum} }$$

🔹 10. Final Closure Statement

$$\boxed{ \text{Mass = Vacuum Selection + Coupling Structure} }$$

Structural Summary
