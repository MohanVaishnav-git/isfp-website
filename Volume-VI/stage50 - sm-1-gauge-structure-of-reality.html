STAGE 50 — MATHEMATICAL STRUCTURE SUMMARY

Gauge Structure of Reality

🔷 1. SYMMETRY SELECTION

$\boxed{ G = SU(3)_C \times SU(2)_L \times U(1)_Y }$

🔷 2. LIE ALGEBRA DECOMPOSITION

$\boxed{ \mathfrak{g} = \mathfrak{su}(3) \oplus \mathfrak{su}(2) \oplus \mathfrak{u}(1) }$

🔷 3. GENERATOR STRUCTURE

$T = \{T^a_C,\; T^i_L,\; Y\}$

$[T^a, T^b] = i f^{abc} T^c$

🔷 4. GAUGE FIELD

$A_\mu = G_\mu^a T^a + W_\mu^i T^i + B_\mu Y$

🔷 5. COVARIANT DERIVATIVE

$\boxed{ D_\mu = \partial_\mu + i g_3 G_\mu^a T^a + i g_2 W_\mu^i T^i + i g_1 B_\mu Y }$

🔷 6. FIELD STRENGTH

$F_{\mu\nu} = \partial_\mu A_\nu - \partial_\nu A_\mu + i [A_\mu, A_\nu]$

🔷 7. COMPONENT FORM

\begin{aligned} G_{\mu\nu}^a &= \partial_\mu G_\nu^a - \partial_\nu G_\mu^a + g_3 f^{abc} G_\mu^b G_\nu^c \\ W_{\mu\nu}^i &= \partial_\mu W_\nu^i - \partial_\nu W_\mu^i + g_2 \epsilon^{ijk} W_\mu^j W_\nu^k \\ B_{\mu\nu} &= \partial_\mu B_\nu - \partial_\nu B_\mu \end{aligned}

🔷 8. GAUGE LAGRANGIAN

$\boxed{ \mathcal{L}_{\text{gauge}} = -\frac{1}{4} G_{\mu\nu}^a G^{a\mu\nu} -\frac{1}{4} W_{\mu\nu}^i W^{i\mu\nu} -\frac{1}{4} B_{\mu\nu} B^{\mu\nu} }$

🔷 9. MATTER INTERACTION

$\boxed{ \mathcal{L}_{\text{int}} = \bar{\psi} \gamma^\mu D_\mu \psi }$

🔷 10. CHARGE DEFINITIONS

$T^a \psi = \lambda^a \psi$

$\boxed{ \text{Charge} = \lambda^a }$

$\boxed{ Q = T^3 + \frac{Y}{2} }$

🔷 11. ALGEBRAIC DYNAMICS

[T^a, T^b] = i f^{abc} T^c

$\boxed{ f^{abc} \neq 0 \Rightarrow \text{non-linear (self-interacting)} }$

$\boxed{ f^{abc} = 0 \Rightarrow \text{linear} }$

🔷 12. FINAL STRUCTURAL EQUATION

$\boxed{ D_\mu = \partial_\mu + \sum_{i} i g_i A_\mu^{(i)} T^{(i)} }$

🔷 FINAL RESULT

$\boxed{ SU(3)\times SU(2)\times U(1) \;\Rightarrow\; \text{complete gauge structure of interactions} }$

🔷 WHAT STAGE 50 ESTABLISHES (MATHEMATICAL)

🔷 STAGE 50 — CLOSURE / COMPLETION THEOREM

Gauge Structure of Reality

🔷 THEOREM (Stage 50 Completion)

Let

$G = SU(3)_C \times SU(2)_L \times U(1)_Y$

be a local gauge symmetry acting on fields $\psi \in R(G)$.

Then the following structure uniquely determines the full interaction framework:

🔷 GIVEN

1. Local Gauge Invariance: $\psi(x) \rightarrow U(x)\psi(x), \quad U(x) \in G$

2. Covariant Derivative: $D_\mu = \partial_\mu + \sum_i i g_i A_\mu^{(i)} T^{(i)}$

3. Field Strength: $F_{\mu\nu} = \partial_\mu A_\nu - \partial_\nu A_\mu + i [A_\mu, A_\nu]$

🔷 THEN (STRUCTURAL CONSEQUENCES)

(i) Sector Decomposition: $G = \prod_i G_i \;\Rightarrow\; \mathcal{L} = \bigoplus_i \mathcal{L}_{G_i}$

(ii) Force Dynamics from Algebra: $$[T^a, T^b] = i f^{abc} T^c; f^{abc} \neq 0 \Rightarrow \text{non-linear dynamics}; f^{abc} = 0 \Rightarrow \text{linear dynamics}$$

(iv) Interaction Structure: $\mathcal{L}_{\text{int}} = \bar{\psi} \gamma^\mu D_\mu \psi$

(v) Gauge Lagrangian: $\mathcal{L}_{\text{gauge}} = -\frac{1}{4} \sum_i F_{\mu\nu}^{(i)} F^{(i)\mu\nu}$

🔷 COMPLETION STATEMENT

$\boxed{ \left(G,\; D_\mu,\; F_{\mu\nu},\; R(G)\right) \;\Rightarrow\; \text{complete gauge interaction structure} }$
